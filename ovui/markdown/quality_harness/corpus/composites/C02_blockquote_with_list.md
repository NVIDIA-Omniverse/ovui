A founder's rule of thumb, from Paul Graham's essay on product-market fit:

> Launch fast, because the sooner you have real users, the sooner you get real feedback. The specific things to watch:
>
> - **Retention** over vanity metrics.
> - **Time-to-first-value** under sixty seconds.
> - **Signal in support tickets**, not just volume.
>
> *If any of these trend the wrong way, ignore everything else.*
